Why now

In 2025, the big platforms rolled back their hate-speech protections. GLAAD's 2026 Social Media Safety Index gives them historically low scores for protecting LGBTQ+ users. The moderation that used to catch the worst comments is being switched off — right as it is needed most.

Hate online rarely floods everything evenly — it lands hard and concentrated on one thread, one page, one person, often the people least equipped to carry it. Lovefilter is for exactly that moment: it gives you back your own shield, without silencing the conversation for everyone else.

How it works

AI reads each comment

It hides hate, harassment, threats and dehumanisation. Criticism, questions and honest disagreement — even harsh ones — stay.

You set the threshold

Mild, medium or strict. Your choice, stored only in your browser.

No silent censorship

Every hidden comment is shown with a reason, so you can always see what was hidden and why. On any technical error a comment is kept, never hidden.

Private by design

No accounts, no profiles, no tracking, no ads. Comment text is sent only to classify it, and is not stored.

Honest about limits: Lovefilter reduces what you see. It does not silence the debate for others, and it is not a safety service — it cannot stop threats being made, and it does not replace reporting serious threats to the police.
